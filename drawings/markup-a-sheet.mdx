<Warning>
**Your markup is not saved until you press Save.** Backing out of the sheet or locking your phone loses it. This is the most common complaint about the drawings module and it's entirely avoidable.
</Warning>

## The tools

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pen" icon="pen">
    Freehand. Circle the thing you're asking about.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Highlighter" icon="highlighter">
    Draw attention without covering the linework underneath.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Box" icon="square">
    A clean rectangle around an area — reads better than a scribble when it's going out to the GC.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Measure" icon="ruler">
    Distance on the sheet. **Needs to be calibrated first** or the numbers are meaningless.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
