<Warning>
Search looks at the **sheet number and title only**. It does not read the text inside the drawing. Searching for a door tag or a room name will not find the sheet it's drawn on.
</Warning>

## Three ways to get to a sheet

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Fastest when you know the sheet number — `A-201`, or part of the title.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Folders" icon="folder">
    Best when you want a whole discipline package — all the structural sheets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Page through" icon="arrow-right">
    Best when you're not sure what you're looking for and want to walk the set.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Get in the habit of searching by sheet number. It's the one thing that's always spelled the same way on every job.
</Tip>
