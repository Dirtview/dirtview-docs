<Note>
**The short version.** The `25 / 25` pager at the edge of the viewer walks you through the **drawing set** — sheet 1 to sheet 25. It is not the revision history of the sheet you're looking at.
</Note>

## Getting around

| To do this | On a phone | On a computer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move around the sheet | Drag with one finger | Click and drag |
| Zoom in | Pinch out | Scroll, or the zoom control |
| Next or previous sheet | The pager arrows | The pager arrows |
