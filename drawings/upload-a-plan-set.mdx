<Note>
**The short version.** Upload the entire plan set as one PDF. DirtView splits it into individual sheets and reads the sheet number and title off the drawing itself. You don't upload sheets one at a time.
</Note>

## Folders are optional, sheets are not

Every page becomes a sheet whether you organize it or not. Folders are just a way to group sheets into discipline packages — architectural, structural, mechanical — so people can find things faster. A job with zero folders and 25 sheets is perfectly normal.

<Tip>
If your tile reads `0 folders / 25 sheets`, nothing is wrong. You just haven't grouped them.
</Tip>
