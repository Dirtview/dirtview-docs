<Note>
**The short version.** Ask a question in your own words and get an answer **with the section it came from**. Always look at the citation — that's the part you can act on.
</Note>

## How to ask well

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Works well" icon="circle-check">
    "What's the required concrete strength for the footings?"

    "Does the spec allow a substitution for the specified hardware?"
  </Card>
  <Card title="Works poorly" icon="circle-xmark">
    "Is this okay?" — no context.

    Questions the book doesn't answer, like schedule or cost.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Read the citation, not just the answer

<Warning>
When a decision costs money or affects life safety, open the section it cited and read it yourself. The answer is a fast way to find the right paragraph — it is not a substitute for having read it.
</Warning>

If what you need isn't in the book, that's not a Spec Ask question. That's an [RFI](/approvals/write-an-rfi).
