<Warning>
**This is the thing nobody guesses.** The Specifications screen shows two lists — **Uploaded** and **Published**. Uploading a spec book does almost nothing on its own. **Publishing** is what makes it searchable and available to attach to submittals.
</Warning>

## What each list means

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Uploaded" icon="cloud-arrow-up">
    The file is in DirtView and is being read and indexed. It is not usable yet. Nobody can search it, and you can't attach its sections to anything.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Published" icon="circle-check">
    The book is live. Sections are searchable, they can be attached to submittals, and Spec Ask can answer against them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
