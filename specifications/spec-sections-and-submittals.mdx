<Note>
**The short version.** The spec section you pick on a submittal comes straight out of your **published** spec book. If the list is empty, the book isn't published.
</Note>

## Why a submittal has a spec section

The section is what ties the thing you're submitting to the requirement it has to meet. It's how a reviewer knows what to check the submittal against, and it's how you build a submittal register that covers the whole book instead of whatever people remembered.

→ [Creating a submittal](/approvals/create-a-submittal)
