<Note>
**The short version.** Mail from DirtView comes **from a person** — so replies go to a human. **Notification History** is the delivery record.
</Note>

## Mail comes from you

Because it's from a real person, the recipient can reply and it goes somewhere. It also means your name is on it. Write like it.

## Notification History is your proof of delivery

This is the log of what DirtView actually sent and to whom. It's the answer to "we never got that submittal."

<Tip>
Before you argue about whether something was sent, check Notification History. It settles it in ten seconds, either way.
</Tip>
