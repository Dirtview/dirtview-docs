<Warning>
**A distribution group is not the project team.** The project team is who can work on the job. A distribution group is a named list of people who receive things. Adding someone to a group does not give them access to the job, and adding them to the job does not put them in any group.
</Warning>

## What it's for

Instead of picking the same eight people every time you send a transmittal, you make a group called "Owner and design team" once and pick that.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the group and name it for its purpose">
    Name it after the job it does — "Submittal reviewers," "Owner monthly" — not after the people in it. People change; purposes don't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the members">
    Employees and external contacts both. → [Employees vs. External Contacts](/people/employees-vs-contacts)
  </Step>
