<Note>
**The short version.** The counters count by **type**, so photos plus documents won't always add up to your total files.
</Note>

## Why the numbers don't add up

A file is counted once as a file, and separately in its type bucket. Anything that isn't a photo or a document — an archive, a spreadsheet, a video — lands in the total without landing in either of the two counts people look at.
