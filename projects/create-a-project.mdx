<Note>
**The short version.** Get the project number and the contractor/owner right at the start. Everything downstream — RFI numbers, submittal logs, notifications — hangs off them.
</Note>

## Fill it out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Project name and number">
    Use the number your company already uses on paperwork. If the GC has their own number for the job, put yours here and keep theirs in the description — one number per field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contractor and owner">
    Two different parties. The **contractor** is who's building it; the **owner** is who it belongs to when it's done. Getting these backwards shows up on every document you send out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Job site">
    The physical address. This is what drives location fields on forms and tickets, so it's worth getting right.
