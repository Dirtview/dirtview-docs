<Warning>
**Read this first.** Adding a person to your company creates their login. It does **not** put them on any job. This is the single most common setup mistake in DirtView.
</Warning>

## The two steps people collapse into one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add them to the company (once)">
    Left menu → **Employees**. This creates their account and gives them a company role.

    → [Adding an employee](/people/add-an-employee)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them to each job (every time)">
    Open the job → project team → add the person. Until you do this, they sign in successfully and see nothing.

    {/* TODO: confirm the exact path and label for adding a person to a project team. */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Company role vs. what they can do here

Their **company role** (Foreman, PM, Admin…) is the ceiling — the set of things that role is ever allowed to do. Being on this job is what lets them do those things *on this job*.

<Tip>
So: "Why can't José see the drawings?" is almost always step 2 missing, not a permissions problem. Check job membership before you start editing roles.
</Tip>
