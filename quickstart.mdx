No setup, no installation. If somebody invited you, you're ten minutes from being useful.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use the email address your office invited. Set your password from the invite link.

    Want everything in Spanish? Switch it now — your profile → **Español**. → [Signing in and language](/orientation/signing-in-and-language)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the one thing that clears up everything else">
    The menu on the left is your **whole company**. The tiles inside a job belong to **that job only**.

    Two minutes: [How DirtView is organized](/orientation/how-dirtview-is-organized)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your job">
    Left menu → **Projects** → your job. Don't see it? You've been added to the company but not to the job — ask your PM. → [Putting people on a project](/projects/add-people-to-a-project)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a drawing">
    The **Drawings** tile → search the sheet number → pinch to zoom.

    → [Finding a sheet fast](/drawings/find-a-sheet)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do one real thing">
    Pick whichever fits your day:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Ask a question" icon="circle-question" href="/approvals/write-an-rfi">
        Write an RFI — or [talk it in](/ai/talk-an-rfi-into-existence).
      </Card>
      <Card title="Write up extra work" icon="ticket" href="/tm-tickets/create-a-ticket">
        A T&M ticket, signed before you leave site.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Fill out your daily log" icon="calendar-day" href="/forms/fill-out-a-form">
        And submit it — a draft doesn't count.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Set your crew up" icon="user-plus" href="/projects/add-people-to-a-project">
        If you're the one doing the setting up.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Two things that trip up nearly everybody:**

1. A **draft** is not submitted. Nobody is notified and nobody is waiting on it.
2. Adding someone to your **company** does not put them on a **job**.
</Warning>
