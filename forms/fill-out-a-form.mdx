<Warning>
**A draft is not turned in.** Nobody is notified and nobody is waiting on it. If you started a form and walked away, it is sitting in your drafts and the office does not have it.
</Warning>

## Fill it out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the form">
    Submissions tab → new → choose the template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in what you know">
    Required fields are marked. You can save as a draft at any point and come back — useful when you're waiting on a delivery ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your photos">
    Attach them here, on the form, not in a text message. On the form they're part of the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Now it's real, and the people set up to be notified get notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Draft vs. Submitted

| | Draft | Submitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who can see it | Just you | The project team |
| Notifications sent | None | Yes |
| Counts on the tile as | `draft` | `submitted` |
| Can you still change it | Yes | To be confirmed |
