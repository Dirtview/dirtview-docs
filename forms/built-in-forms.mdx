## What ships

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Daily Field Log" icon="calendar-day">
    Crew, hours, weather, work performed, photos. The daily record of what happened on site.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transmittal" icon="paper-plane">
