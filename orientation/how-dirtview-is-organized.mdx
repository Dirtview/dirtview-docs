<Note>
**The short version.** The menu down the left side is your **whole company**. Everything inside a project belongs to **that one job**.
</Note>

## Two levels, and that's it

DirtView has exactly two levels. Almost every "where is that?" question is really a question about which level you're on.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Company level" icon="building">
    The left-hand menu. Your people, their roles, your list of jobs. Set up once, used by every job.

    Employees · External Contacts · Roles · Projects · Settings
  </Card>
  <Card title="Project level" icon="helmet-safety">
    The tiles you see after you open a job. These only exist inside that job.

    Drawings · Specifications · RFIs · Submittals · Change Orders · Forms · T&M Tickets · Documents & Media
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why this matters on your first day

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I added someone to the company but they can't see the job">
    Correct — and that's on purpose. Adding a person at the company level creates their login. It does not put them on any job. You add them to each job separately.

    → [Putting people on a project](/projects/add-people-to-a-project)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm looking for drawings in the left menu and they're not there">
    Drawings live inside a job, because every job has a different set. Open the project first, then pick the **Drawings** tile.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The numbers on my Dashboard don't match this job">
    The Dashboard counts everything across your company, not the job you last had open.

    → [Reading your Dashboard](/orientation/your-dashboard)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
