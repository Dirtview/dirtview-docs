<Note>
**The short version.** Anything you do standing in the dirt works on the phone. Anything you set up once for everyone is easier at a desk.
</Note>

## Do this on your phone

- Take photos and attach them where they belong
- Talk an RFI or a form entry in instead of typing — see [Talking an RFI into existence](/ai/talk-an-rfi-into-existence)
- Fill out a Daily Field Log or a T&M ticket at the end of the shift
- Get a signature from the GC before you leave the site — see [Getting a signature](/tm-tickets/get-a-signature)
- Pull up a sheet to check a dimension — see [The sheet viewer](/drawings/sheet-viewer)

## Do this at a desk

- Build or change a form template — see [Building a template](/forms/build-a-template)
- Set up roles and permissions — see [Custom roles](/people/custom-roles)
- Upload a plan set or a spec book
- Import projects from Procore
- Review and approve anything with money on it

<Tip>
Both work either way. This is about what's comfortable, not what's allowed. Nobody should be building a 20-field template on a phone in a truck.
</Tip>
