<Note>
**The short version.** DirtView runs fully in English or Spanish — the menus, the forms, and the voice features. You pick your own language, and it doesn't change anyone else's.
</Note>

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open DirtView">
    Go to the DirtView web address your company gave you, or open the app on your phone.

    {/* TODO: confirm the production sign-in URL and whether there is a native app or a mobile web app. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email you were invited with">
    Use the same email address your office used to invite you. A different address won't find your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password the first time">
    Your invite email has a link to set your password. That link is only good for your account — don't forward it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Never share a login.** Every action in DirtView is recorded against the person who did it — who wrote an RFI, who signed a ticket. A shared login makes that record worthless when you need it for a claim.
</Warning>

## Switch to Spanish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Your name or picture, top corner of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the language setting">
    Pick **Español**. The page reloads in Spanish.

    {/* TODO: confirm the exact location and label of the language control (profile menu vs. Settings) and the wording used in the app. */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
This is your setting alone. Your foreman can work in Spanish while the PM works in English, on the same job, on the same RFI.
</Tip>
