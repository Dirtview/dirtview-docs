## The counts are company-wide

If your Dashboard says 12 open RFIs, that's 12 across every active job — not 12 on the job you had open last. To see one job's numbers, open the job and read its [Project Dashboard](/projects/project-dashboard) instead.

## Every card is a door

Don't read the Dashboard and then go hunting through the menu. Click the number. It takes you to the list it was counting, with the filter already applied.
