**The short version.** Two extractors. One reads your spec book and proposes the submittal register. One reads a certification card and pulls out the expiry date.
</Note>

## Submittal Extract

Building a submittal register by hand means somebody reads the whole spec book and types a few hundred rows. Submittal Extract reads the published book and proposes the register for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish the spec book first">
    Nothing works against an unpublished book. → [Uploaded vs. Published](/specifications/upload-a-spec-book)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the extract">
    It works through the sections and proposes submittal items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what came back before you send anything">
    This is the step. Check for sections it missed, items that don't apply to your scope, and duplicates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the register">
    Now you have a register that covers the book instead of covering what people remembered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A register you haven't read is not a register. Review it against your own scope of work — the spec book covers the whole project, and you're one trade.
</Warning>

## Certifications Extract

Point it at a photo of a card and it pulls out the expiry date, so you're not typing dates off a phone photo.

Always check the date it read. → [Certifications and expiry tracking](/people/certifications)
