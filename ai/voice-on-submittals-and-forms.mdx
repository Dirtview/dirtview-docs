## Tables need checking

A labor table or an items table is rows and columns, and speech is a stream. It does a reasonable job of splitting "Mike, carpenter, eight hours; Luis, laborer, ten hours" into rows — but check every row before submitting, especially the numbers.

<Warning>
Hours and quantities are what get billed. Read the table back before you submit, every time.
</Warning>

## Fixing it without starting over

You don't have to clear the whole thing and re-record. Tap into the field or row that's wrong and fix just that.
