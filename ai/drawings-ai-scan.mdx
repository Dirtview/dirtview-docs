## What it will not catch

<Warning>
Be blunt about this with your crews:

- Hand annotations and field sketches on a scanned sheet
- Anything illegible, skewed or photographed at an angle
- A conflict between two sheets — that's a human judgement, and it's an [RFI](/approvals/write-an-rfi)
</Warning>
