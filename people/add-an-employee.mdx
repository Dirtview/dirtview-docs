## Deactivate, never delete

<Warning>
When somebody leaves, set them **Inactive**. Their RFIs, their signed tickets and their form submissions are your project record — and on a claim, a record signed by a person who no longer exists in the system is a problem you don't want to explain.
</Warning>

Inactive does the one thing you actually need: they can't sign in any more.
