<Note>
**The short version.** Upload a photo of the card. DirtView reads the expiry date and warns you before it lapses.
</Note>

## Upload a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the employee">
    Left menu → Employees → the person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the certification and upload a photo of the card">
    A clear, flat photo. The expiry date gets read off the card.

    → How the extraction works: [Certifications Extract](/ai/extract-tools)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the date it read">
    Always check. A misread expiry date is worse than no date, because you stop watching for it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
