## Start from an existing role

Never start from nothing. Pick the built-in role closest to what you want and adjust it — you inherit a sensible baseline instead of assembling one checkbox at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate the closest built-in role">
    Want a "Foreman who can also approve tickets"? Start from Foreman.

  <Step title="Name it for the job, not the person">
    "Field Approver," not "Mike's role." Mike will move on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change only what you came to change">
    Then stop. Every extra permission is one you'll have to justify later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the matrix row by row before saving">
    The matrix is a grid of what this role can do in each module. Read it, don't skim it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with one person for a week">
    Before you move thirty people onto it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
