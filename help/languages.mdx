<Note>
**Everyone picks their own language.** Your foreman can work entirely in Spanish while the PM works in English — on the same job, on the same RFI, at the same time.
</Note>

## What is translated

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Translated" icon="circle-check">
    The app itself — menus, buttons, field labels, statuses, notifications, and the voice features.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Not translated" icon="circle-info">
    What people type and what people upload. An RFI written in Spanish stays in Spanish. Drawings and specs are whatever the design team issued.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Voice works in both

You can talk an RFI in in Spanish and have it come out as a written RFI. → [Talking an RFI into existence](/ai/talk-an-rfi-into-existence)

<Warning>
Read the draft before you send it. If you dictated in Spanish and the RFI is going out in English, check that the sheet numbers, dimensions and quantities came across exactly.
</Warning>

## For admins setting up a bilingual crew

<Steps>
  <Step title="Let people set their own language">
    Don't set it for them. It's a personal setting and it's one less thing for you to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your templates in both languages where it matters">
    Field labels are translated; **your own** wording in an instructional-text field isn't. On a form a Spanish-speaking crew fills out daily, put both languages in the label. → [Building a template](/forms/build-a-template)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Spanish videos to the field">
    Series 00, 40, 60 and 90.1 are recorded in Spanish as well as English — the field-facing ones. → [Video library](/video-library)
  </Step>
</Steps>
