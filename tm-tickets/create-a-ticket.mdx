<Note>
**The short version.** Six steps, in order. Fill it out on site, the day the work happens, and get it signed before you leave.
</Note>

## The six steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Project info">
    Which job, what date, and what the extra work was. Write what happened in plain words — "removed 40 LF of unmarked conduit at the north footing," not "extra work per direction."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Labor">
    Who, what trade, how many hours. Names, not just a headcount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Material">
    What you used and how much. Quantities with units.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Other costs">
    Equipment, rentals, disposal, delivery. This is the step people skip, and it's real money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attachments">
    Photos of the condition **before** you fixed it, plus delivery tickets. Photos taken after the fact prove nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    A signature from whoever directed the work, on site. → [Getting a signature](/tm-tickets/get-a-signature)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can leave blank

Material and other costs genuinely don't apply to some tickets. Labor, the description and the signature always do.
