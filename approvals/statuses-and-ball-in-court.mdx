**The single most confused thing in DirtView.** Status and Ball in Court are not the same field and do not move together.
</Warning>

## Two questions, two answers

## How they move apart

An RFI can sit at **In Review** for eight days while the ball moves from the architect, to the structural engineer, and back to the architect. The status never changed. Three different people were on the hook.

That's why:

- **"What's the status?"** tells you how far along it is.
- **"Who's got the ball?"** tells you who to call this morning.

<Tip>
In a coordination meeting, read the ball in court column, not the status column. Status tells you nothing about who's late.
</Tip>

## The statuses

| Status | What it means | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Submitted, not picked up yet | Watch the ball in court |
| In Review | Somebody is working on it | Chase whoever holds the ball |
| Answered | You have an answer | Read it, and act or push back |
| Closed | Done, on the record | Nothing — it's evidence now |
