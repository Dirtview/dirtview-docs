## The three numbers

  </Card>
  <Card title="Version" icon="code-branch">

## Create it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the spec section">
    From your published spec book. If the list is empty, the book isn't published. → [Uploaded vs. Published](/specifications/upload-a-spec-book)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or override the suggested number">
    DirtView suggests the next number. Override it if your GC or the owner requires their own numbering — but then be consistent for the whole job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the package">
    Product data, shop drawings, samples documentation — whatever the section requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the review routing">
    → [Review Routing](/approvals/review-routing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    A draft submittal is not submitted, and the clock hasn't started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which iteration the list shows

The submittal log shows one row per item — the current iteration, not every version you ever sent. To see the earlier ones, open the submittal and look at its versions.
