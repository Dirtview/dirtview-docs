<Warning>
**In Review and Outstanding are not the same thing.** In Review means somebody has it and is working on it. Outstanding means it's still owed — nobody has it yet.
</Warning>

## The four states

| State | Meaning | Who's holding it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding | Still owed. Not sent, or sent and not picked up. | You, usually |
| In Review | A reviewer has it and is working on it | The reviewer |
| Approved | Cleared. You can build or order to it. | Nobody |
| Rejected | Not cleared. Fix it and resubmit as a new revision. | You |
