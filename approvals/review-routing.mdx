<Note>
**The short version.** Routing is the order people see the item. Anyone who already responded is **locked** — their response is part of the record and can't be edited away.
</Note>

## Set up the route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add reviewers in the order they should see it">
    Usually: the person who can actually answer, then whoever needs it for the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it short">
    Every reviewer is a day. Two reviewers who can decide beats five who can comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder before you send, not after">
    Rearranging mid-review is where things get confusing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Locked reviewers

<Warning>
Once somebody has responded, you cannot remove or change them. Their response is evidence. This is deliberate — it's what makes the review history worth anything in a dispute.
</Warning>

## Changing the reviewer who holds the ball

<Warning>
If you change the reviewer **currently holding** the item, it comes back to you. That's not a bug — the item can't be in someone's court and reassigned at the same time, so it lands back with you to re-send.

Expect this and plan for it: swapping the current reviewer costs you a round trip.
</Warning>
